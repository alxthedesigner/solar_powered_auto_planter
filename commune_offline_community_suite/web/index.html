Expand Your Knowledge and Build Community

Commune is an offline library that gives you the hands-on skills to build a self-sufficient community that can work together for the public good. This system is built for portability and to avoid subscriptions, hidden data collection, restrictions, and full reliance on the internet.

Field identification

Scan plants, animals, writing in other languages, and electrical or mechanical parts with the Commune field identifier. It looks them up in your library and tells you what you can do with them. Each upload lands here, split into what it identified and what still needs identifying, with room for your notes.

Planter

The solar planter waters itself from its rainwater tank when the soil gets dry. Its readings (soil moisture, temperature, battery, tank) arrive here through the planter bridge, and you choose how it waters each zone.

Planter

Readings from the solar planter, the watering it did, and the settings it follows. They're kept on this computer. Settings reach the planter through its bridge, its SD card or its phone page.

How the planter reaches Commune

Any of these works on its own. The planter keeps watering with none of them.

Wi-Fi bridgeA Raspberry Pi that collects readings all day. Commune syncs with it every 2 minutes while it's open.
SD cardWorks with no power or network connection. Plug the planter's SDcard into this computer.
Phone pageWorks with no internet connection. Press the connect button on the planter, join the planter's local network Planter-… (password SETUP_PASSWORD in planter_config.h), and its settings page opens. Changes made there show up here the next time Commune hears from the planter.

What happens to the drive

We add one folder, OfflineLibrary. Nothing else on the drive is changed or deleted.

Best format: exFAT. It works on Mac, Windows and Linux and holds big files.

Which computers should open this drive?

We put a small reader app on the drive for each one you tick (about 10 MB each). Ticking them all is safest: you may not know what computer you'll find.

Phones and tablets don't need this. Install the free Kiwix app, then open the files in the drive's zim folder.

Using the drive with no internet

1 · Unplug safely

Stop the reader, then eject the drive before pulling it out.

2 · Plug into any computer

Mac, Windows or Linux. No internet or install needed.

3 · Double-click START

Open the OfflineLibrary folder and pick yours:

START-Mac.command START-Windows.bat START-Linux.sh

4 · Read in the browser

The home page opens with a link for every topic and library on the drive.

A README-FIRST.txt file with these same steps is saved on the drive too.
